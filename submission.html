What fan problem are you solving?

Whenever my friends and I play private fantasy leagues during live cricket matches, we keep switching between the match and the fantasy app just to check our points and leaderboard positions. Every wicket or six means refreshing the app and figuring out who gained points and who took the lead. It gets frustrating because you're constantly checking numbers instead of enjoying the match. The leaderboard tells you what changed, but not why it matters to your rivalry.

How does your solution help?

I built FanLens AI, an Android companion app that lets fans track their fantasy league without leaving the live match. Its main feature is Googly, a draggable cricket-ball mascot that floats over the screen and displays the live score, fantasy rank, and points at a glance.

Tap Googly, and FanLens AI explains what's happening in your league in plain English. For example, it can explain how Bumrah's wicket affected your friend's score and what Kohli needs to do for you to regain the top spot.

The idea is simple: make fantasy cricket more engaging by connecting what's happening on the field with what's happening in your league.

Which AI tools did you use?

  • OpenRouter with NVIDIA Nemotron: Turns verified score changes into natural-language rivalry insights.
  • Fact-grounded AI validation: The server calculates and verifies all points and score changes. The AI only explains this verified data, reducing the risk of fabricated statistics or misleading insights.
